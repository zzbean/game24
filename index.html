How to Play

Goal: Be the first player to shed all your cards.

Each round: 4 cards are dealt — Player 1's 2 on top, Player 2's 2 on bottom. Use all four values with +, −, ×, ÷ to make 24. Ace = 1.

Claiming: First to figure out a solution clicks I Have It! and states it aloud. The other player clicks Accepted or Wrong. If wrong, the other player may then claim.

Winning a round: The solver sheds their 2 cards. The other player absorbs all 4 cards on the table plus any pile.

No solution: Click No Solution This Round — all 4 cards go to the pile for the next round's loser.
